Welcome to Nest Guesthouse

An Elegant Oasis Between Garda & Verona

Nest Guesthouse was born from the desire to offer an authentic, relaxing, and private stay in the heart of Castelnuovo del Garda. Set in a stunning Italian villa surrounded by lush greenery, our guesthouse combines the warm, welcoming feel of a private home with modern hospitality amenities.

Whether you are planning a family trip to Gardaland, a romantic getaway to Lake Garda, or a relaxing stay with your four-legged friends, Nest Guesthouse is the ideal starting point. We offer spacious, elegantly furnished rooms, private or shared kitchens, and a lush Mediterranean garden with a relaxing lounge area.
